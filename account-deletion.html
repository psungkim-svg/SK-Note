Delete your soonenote account

You can permanently delete your account and synced data from the web app:

  1. Open soonenote.
  2. Go to Settings → Account · Sync.
  3. Sign in, select Delete account, and confirm by typing DELETE.

This deletes your Supabase sign-in account and synced notes and folders. Vault notes stay only on the device and can be removed with Settings → Delete all or by clearing the site’s local data.

If you cannot sign in, use the password-reset option in the app first. The deletion feature requires the app operator’s delete-account server function to be deployed.
